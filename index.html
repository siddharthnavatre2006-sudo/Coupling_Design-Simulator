    Aim

    To design a rigid flange coupling and a flexible bushed-pin coupling for a given power and speed, to check every component against its allowable stress, and to study how service factor, material strength and shaft misalignment change the safety factor and torque capacity.

    Objectives

    • Determine the shaft diameter from the design torque.
    • Proportion the hub, flange, key, bolts or pins by standard rules.
    • Find the stresses and the safety factor of each element.
    • Observe the effect of parallel and angular misalignment on both coupling types.

    Theory

    A coupling joins two shafts to transmit torque. A rigid coupling gives a solid connection and needs precisely aligned shafts. A flexible coupling uses elastic elements (bushes on pins) that absorb small misalignment, shock and vibration.

    1. Torque and design torque

    T = 9550 · P / n (N·m, P in kW, n in rpm) Td = Ks · T (Ks = service factor)

    2. Shaft diameter (torsion)

    Td = (π/16) · τs · d³ → d = [16·Td / (π·τs)]^(1/3) (rounded to a standard size)

    3. Standard proportions (Indian-textbook practice)

    Hub dia Dh = 2d Flange thickness t = 0.5d Bolt PCD D1 = 3d Flange outer dia D2 = 4d Key b = d/4, h = d/6, length l = 1.5d

    4. Stresses checked

    Key shear τk = 2T / (d·b·l) Key crushing σc = 4T / (d·h·l) Hub shear τh = 16·T·Dh / [π(Dh⁴ − d⁴)] Flange shear τf = 2T / (π·Dh²·t)

    5. Rigid coupling – bolts in shear

    T = n · (π/4)·db² · τb · (D1/2) → db = √[8T / (π·n·τb·D1)]

    6. Flexible coupling – pins and bushes

    Load per pin F = 2T / (n·D1) Bush pressure p = F / (dp · lb), lb = 1.5·dp Pin bending σb = 32·F·(lb/2 + 5) / (π·dp³) Pin shear τ = 4F / (π·dp²) Combined σeq = √(σb² + 3τ²) ≤ √3 · τallow

    7. Misalignment and safety

    Utilisation r = e/eallow + α/αallow. Stresses are multiplied by Km = 1 + 1.5r (rigid) or 1 + 0.3r (flexible; hub and flange 1 + 0.1r). This is a simplified model for learning. Safety factor SF = allowable stress ÷ actual stress. Rim speed v = π·D2·n/60000 is limited to 30 m/s.

    Torque capacity = Td × (minimum SF over all stress checks) SAFE: SF ≥ 1.5 MARGINAL: 1.0 – 1.5 FAIL: SF < 1.0

    Assumptions

    • Steady torque with service factor covering shock; the torque is shared equally by all bolts or pins.
    • Fatigue, stress concentration at keyways and thermal effects are not modelled.
    • Use for learning and preliminary sizing; verify final designs against the standards and the manufacturer’s data.
